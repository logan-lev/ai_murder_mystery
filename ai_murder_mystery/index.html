    How to play

    Commander Vale is dead and one of his four crew killed him. There's a new case every day, and everyone gets the same one.

    • Search rooms for evidence.
    • Question the crew. Put actions in parentheses, like (lean in).
    • Show evidence to a suspect to break their story.

    Every search, question, or piece of evidence you show costs one turn. When the storm clears, you have to make your accusation.

    You only get one accusation: the killer, their motive, and up to three pieces of evidence that prove it.

    Tip: everyone lies about where they were when the Commander died, but only one of them is lying about murder. Find out what the others were hiding.

    The crew are played by an AI that runs entirely in your browser. The first visit downloads the model, which takes a while. After that it's cached.
