    How to play

    Someone aboard Erebus Station has been murdered, and one of the five crew members questioned did it. There's a new case every day with a new victim, and everyone gets the same one.

    • Search rooms for evidence.
    • Question the crew by typing what you'd say.
    • Show evidence to a suspect to break their story.

    Every search, question, or piece of evidence you show uses up one turn. When you run out of turns, the storm clears and you have to make your accusation.

    You only get one accusation: the killer, their motive, and up to three pieces of evidence that prove it.
